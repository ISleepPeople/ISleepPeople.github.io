Delilah Home

A private, self-hosted assistant for one household

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Delilah Home ("Delilah") is a private, non-commercial assistant operated by one family on a self-hosted server in a private residence. This policy explains exactly what data Delilah accesses, why, where it is stored, and how to remove it.

Who this applies to

Only members of the operating household, each of whom connects their own Google account voluntarily. Delilah is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google user data we access

Delilah requests these scopes, and uses them only as described:

ScopeWhy
calendarRead your events to answer questions; create or change events when you ask.
gmail.sendSend a message only when you explicitly ask Delilah to send it.
gmail.readonlyRead your mail only when you ask, in two ways. “Check my email”: Delilah fetches the sender, subject, date and Gmail’s short preview line for your most recent messages (10 by default), or for messages matching a search you give it — it does not open the full message. “Scan my email for packages”: Delilah searches the last 7 days (up to 30 if you ask) for up to 50 shipping-related messages, reads their text to find carrier tracking numbers, and keeps only the tracking number and carrier. This permission is read-only: Delilah cannot delete, change or label your mail with it, and nothing reads your mail in the background.
drive.fileCreate and modify only the files Delilah itself makes, or ones you explicitly hand it. Delilah cannot see the rest of your Drive.
tasksRead and update your task lists.
contacts.readonlyResolve names to addresses so "email Mom" works.

Delilah accesses this data only to fulfil a request made by the account's own owner. One household member's request never reads another member's Google data.

Scopes we deliberately do not request

Delilah requests exactly one of Google's restricted scopes, gmail.readonly, added on 1 October 2026 for the two email features described above. It does not request full Gmail access (gmail.modify or full mailbox control), and it cannot browse your Drive (drive.readonly), which was removed on 25 September 2026 to keep Delilah's access as narrow as the features actually require.

Limited Use disclosure

Delilah’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to answer a request you made, and only for you. Specifically:
  • It is never used to train or fine-tune any AI model. Conversation turns that used your Google data, and replies that repeated it, are never recorded as training data.
  • It is never sent to an outside AI provider. Delilah sometimes escalates a hard question to an external model. A request that needs your Google data is never escalated, and earlier replies that contained your Google data are removed before anything is escalated.
  • It is never sold and never used for advertising. It is not shared with third parties, with one exception you control: when you ask for a package’s status, that package’s tracking number (found by a package scan of your email) is sent to the parcel-tracking service. Nothing else from your email goes with it.
  • It is not read by people, except when you ask us to, when it is needed for security or to comply with the law, or for operating and troubleshooting the household server.

Where data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted with Fernet (AES) before being written, and stored in a PostgreSQL database on the household server. That database is not reachable from the internet.
  • Delilah does not keep a copy of your mailbox, calendar or Drive. When Delilah tells you about an email or an event, that reply is kept on the household server as part of your conversation history, so it can follow up (“who was that from again?”). That history never leaves the server, is never used for training, and is never sent to an outside AI provider. Tracking numbers and carriers found by a package scan are also saved so Delilah can follow those packages. You can ask for any of this to be deleted.
  • Conversation history with the assistant is stored locally so it can remember context. You can ask for it to be deleted.
  • The server is in a private home, protected by encrypted storage and private-network access only.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data — to anyone, ever
  • Share it with advertisers or data brokers, or with any third party except as described in the Limited Use disclosure above
  • Use it to train or improve any AI model, including Delilah’s own
  • Run third-party analytics or tracking
  • Access an account other than in service of its own owner's request

Third parties

The language model answering your requests normally runs locally on the household server. If a request is escalated to an external model provider, only the text of that request and the recent conversation are sent. Requests that need your Google data are never escalated, and earlier replies containing your Google data are removed first. Credentials and tokens are never sent. Because your Google data never reaches these providers, their own data-handling terms never apply to it.

Your control

  • Revoke Delilah's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access stops immediately.
  • Ask the operator to delete your stored tokens and conversation history; this is done on request.
  • Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the account is disconnected.

Children

Delilah is used within one family and may be used by minors under the supervision of their parents or guardians, who operate the system. No data is collected from children for any commercial purpose.
