Delilah Home

A private, self-hosted assistant for one household

Delilah Home is a private assistant that runs entirely on a server in our house. It helps the people who live here keep track of calendars, tasks, messages and media. It is not a product, it is not for sale, and it is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Answers questions and holds conversations using a language model running on our own hardware
  • Reads and creates calendar events for household members who connect their account
  • Reads and creates tasks
  • Sends email on request — it cannot read your mailbox
  • Looks up contacts to resolve names
  • Creates and edits only the Drive files it makes or you hand it — it cannot browse your Drive
  • Controls home automation, media and household logistics

Who can use it

Only members of one family, on our home network or over a private Tailscale connection. There is no public sign-up. Each person chooses whether to connect their own Google account, and can disconnect at any time.

Where it runs

On a single self-hosted Linux server in a private residence. The language model runs locally on that machine. There is no third-party analytics, advertising, or data broker involved.

Your data stays here. Google account data retrieved by Delilah is used only to answer that person's own requests. It is never sold, never shared, and never used to train any model.
